# World & districts

Semi-open Brazilian megacity. Districts connect as story flags unlock.

## Morro Neon

Vertical favela of LED veins, lajes, botecos, and pixo alleys. Starting district.

## Central Fair

24h market with Copacabana-style wave sidewalks, pastel stalls, cane juice, and live odds boards.

## Odds Avenue

Glass towers of Brazilian bet corporations, industrial funk, and security checkpoints.

## Casa Suprema Tower

Megacorp HQ. Final story arena.

Travel is free among unlocked districts. Exploration is tile-based with depth sorting for a fake-3/4 look.
