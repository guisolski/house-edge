# Objectives

## Player goals

1. Clear Rita’s midnight debt clock by climbing the city’s odd ladder.
2. Collect proof that Casa Suprema rigs the national RNG.
3. Trigger a public scandal on Odds Avenue.
4. Beat CEO Augusto Casa in the Tower duel.
5. Optionally grind side jobs for credits, reputation, and upgrades.

## Design goals

- Every meaningful interaction routes through the betting system (stake, odds, house edge, payout).
- Semi-open world: explore unlocked districts freely; story gates the next district.
- Desktop, mobile (touch controls), and gamepad support.
- English UI/docs/code; Brazilian flavor in setting, art, music, and light slang.
