# Betting & grind

## Theme

Bet corporations (led by Casa Suprema) replaced cash prices with wagers. Even water is an odd. The house edge is a narrative villain and a mechanical tax.

## Bet loop

1. Choose a stake (or accept the listed stake).
2. Roll against `winChance` (already includes house edge flavor).
3. On win: payout = stake × (numerator/denominator + 1); reputation up.
4. On lose: stake gone; reputation may drop.
5. Story bets can set flags / unlock districts on success; story beats still advance when the wager resolves affordably.

## Grind loop

Side jobs per district (water rotation, rooftop draws, moto dashes, pastel stalls, deliveries, commissioned pixo) generate credits and reputation.

## Upgrades

- Ipê amulet — luck
- Armored wallet — credit cushion
- Signed pixo — fame
- Homemade filter — cheaper water odds

## Lore note

Informal pre-corp street lotteries were eradicated when the corporations consolidated gambling. In-world draws are licensed products of the Casa — never framed as those old games.
