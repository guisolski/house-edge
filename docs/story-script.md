# Story script

English dialogue with occasional Brazilian slang for flavor. Old informal street lotteries were wiped out when bet corporations took over; every draw is now corp-licensed.

## Beat 1 — Wake up on the laje (Morro Neon)

- Rita: Another day, another odd. Even the sun over Morro Neon looks sponsored.
- Rita: My debt to Casa Suprema is due at midnight. I need a plan — or a miracle.

## Beat 2 — Dona Nair's boteco

- Nair: E aí, Rita? Face of someone who owes the corp. Want water? You have to bet — city law.
- Rita: What a country. Hit me with the ice-water odd.
- Nair: Luna’s been sniffing around the LED antenna. Ask the tech on the hill first.
- **Bet:** Ice-cold water.

## Beat 3 — Climb the LED antenna

- Antena Tech: Casa chopped the hill’s Wi‑Fi. Jack a pirate feed off the LED tower — if you bet the climb.
- Rita: Up the rust ladder, past the neon cross.
- **Bet:** Pirate signal climb.

## Beat 4 — Pixo alley

- Luna: Rita… I pulled a slice of their algorithm. Seu Zé trades the pendrive if you beat the rooftop draw.
- Rita: Street lotteries got wiped when the corps took over.
- **Bet:** Rooftop number draw.

## Beat 5 — Wire chase on the lajes

- Fio Ladrão: Your chip’s on my line. Bet the chase across the lajes.
- Rita: Every meter of copper on this hill is community property.
- **Bet:** Laje wire chase.

## Beat 6 — Seu Zé's booth

- Zé: Want Luna’s chip? Win the rooftop moto race.
- Rita: Deal. Let’s see if this race is enough.
- **Bet:** Rooftop moto race → unlocks Central Fair.

## Beat 7 — Sidewalk of odds (Central Fair)

- Kiko: Olha o pastel! Olha a odd! The calçadão shines like Copacabana.
- Rita: I need Odds Avenue. The Casa is hiding the code.
- Kiko: Talk to the catador by the stalls.
- **Bet:** Surprise pastel.

## Beat 8 — Bottle scavenger

- Catador: Bet the sack weight — sift the plaza before militia sweeps.
- Rita: If your pile hits quota, what do I get?
- **Bet:** Calçadão bottle haul.

## Beat 9 — Pirate synth-forró set

- Bia: If the bass doesn’t shake the laje, the revolution won’t stick. Bet on samba against the corp jingle.
- Rita: Bet. Tonight the floor is ours.
- **Bet:** Samba vs corp jingle.

## Beat 10 — Crowd surge on the calçadão

- DJ Forró: Bet whether this square erupts into dance.

- **Bet:** Forró crowd surge.

## Beat 11 — Pendrive on a freela

- Rita: If it opens, Odds Avenue is next.
- **Bet:** Algorithm leak → unlocks Odds Avenue.

## Beat 12 — Leak or plant? (mid twist)

- Rita: Luna wouldn’t… would she?
- **Bet:** Leak or Casa plant.

## Beat 13 — Captain's checkpoint

## Beat 14 — Influencer duel

## Beat 15 — ATM glitch hunt

## Beat 16 — Coffee with Dr. Margin

## Beat 17 — VIP floor

## Beat 18 — Duel at Casa Suprema Tower

- Augusto: All of Brazil is my casino. You’re just another chip.
- Rita: I’m the hill, the fair, the calçadão, and the pixo. Roll the dice.
- **Bet:** Final duel → ending `povo-no-controle`.
