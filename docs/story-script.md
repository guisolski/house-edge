# Story script

English dialogue with occasional Brazilian slang for flavor. Old informal street lotteries were wiped out when bet corporations took over; every draw is now corp-licensed.

**Timing estimate (v1.15):** ~18–22 minutes for a direct player (was ~12 min in v1.14.1). New beats add meaningful choices and a lockpick mini-game — not padding or backtracking.

## Beat 1 — Wake up on the laje (Morro Neon)

- Rita: Another day, another odd. Even the sun over Morro Neon looks sponsored.
- Rita: My debt to Casa Suprema is due at midnight. I need a plan — or a miracle.

## Beat 2 — Dona Nair's boteco

- Nair: E aí, Rita? Face of someone who owes the corp. Want water? You have to bet — city law.
- Rita: Style choice (Loud / Quiet) — both labels say **then Take the bet**; choices do not loop.
- Nair: Either way, the ice odd is the door.
- **Bet:** Ice-cold water (timing).

## Beat 3 — Spare copper + LED antenna

- Catador: Weigh the bright bag (puzzle).
- Antena Tech: Jack a pirate feed — match the splice tones (hack).

## Beat 4 — Pixo alley + wire chase + Seu Zé

- Luna / Seu Zé / Fio: rooftop draw, chase, moto race → unlocks Central Fair.

## Beat 5 — Central Fair calçadão

- Kiko pastel → Catador bottles → Bia samba → Dancer cue → DJ forró crowd.

## Beat 6 — Casa lockbox under the stage (new)

- Stage Hand: Mid-fair heist. Pick tumblers on a Casa lockbox (lockpick).
- Choice: crack for the hill vs leverage — then radio pirate.
- **Bet:** Casa lockbox (lockpick).

## Beat 7 — Pirate radio → tipster → courier → Luna pendrive

- Radio / Barraca Tia / Lavador / Luna: unlock Odds Avenue.

## Beat 8 — Leak or plant? (mid twist)

## Beat 9 — Checkpoint → courier → influencer → radar

- Capita pixo, courier shadow, influencer read, mensageiro sweep tip.

## Beat 10 — Server-cage infiltration (new)

- Cage Runner: Four tumblers before the sweep returns (lockpick).

## Beat 11 — ATM glitch + lobby cam slip
