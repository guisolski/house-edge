# Story script

English dialogue with occasional Brazilian slang for flavor. Old informal street lotteries were wiped out when bet corporations took over; every draw is now corp-licensed.

## Beat 1 — Wake up on the laje (Morro Neon)

- Rita: Another day, another odd. Even the sun over Morro Neon looks sponsored.
- Rita: My debt to Casa Suprema is due at midnight. I need a plan — or a miracle.

## Beat 2 — Dona Nair's boteco

- Nair: E aí, Rita? Face of someone who owes the corp. Want water? You have to bet — city law.
- Rita: What a country. Hit me with the ice-water odd.
- Nair: Luna found a leak in the Casa RNG. Look for her in the pixo alley.
- **Bet:** Ice-cold water.

## Beat 3 — Pixo alley

- Luna: Rita… I pulled a slice of their algorithm. Seu Zé trades the pendrive if you beat the rooftop draw.
- Rita: Street lotteries got wiped when the corps took over. Now even the laje plays by Casa rules.
- **Bet:** Rooftop number draw.

## Beat 4 — Seu Zé's booth

- Zé: Everything’s licensed now. Want Luna’s chip? Win the rooftop moto race.
- Rita: Deal. If I win, I open the road to the Central Fair.
- Zé: Pode crer. Kiko sells routes with the pastel.
- **Bet:** Rooftop moto race → unlocks Central Fair.

## Beat 5 — Sidewalk of odds (Central Fair)

- Kiko: Olha o pastel! Olha a odd! The calçadão shines like Copacabana, except every stone is a market.
- Rita: I need Odds Avenue. The Casa is hiding the code.
- Kiko: Bia’s pirate set can scare the militia into opening the gate.
- **Bet:** Surprise pastel.

## Beat 6 — Pirate synth-forró set

- Bia: If the bass doesn’t shake the laje, the revolution won’t stick. Bet on samba against the corp jingle.
- Rita: Bet. Tonight the floor is ours.
- **Bet:** Samba vs corp jingle.

## Beat 7 — Pendrive on a freela

- Luna: While the party rolls, we slip the leak through. Bet whether the file opens.
- Rita: It opened. Odds Avenue, I’m coming.
- **Bet:** Algorithm leak → unlocks Odds Avenue.

## Beat 8 — Captain's checkpoint

- Vespera: Papers and chips. No odd, no passage. Or paint the facade.
- Rita: I’ll take pixo over a stamp.
- **Bet:** Pixo challenge.

## Beat 9 — Coffee with Dr. Margin

- Margin: Regulation is just another table. Wager on a public hearing.
- Rita: People will see the house edge eating schools, hospitals, and water.
- Margin: Win the hearing and the Tower elevator unlocks. Lose and the scandal dies in committee.
- **Bet:** Rigged public hearing → unlocks Tower.

## Beat 10 — Duel at Casa Suprema Tower

- Augusto: All of Brazil is my casino. You’re just another chip.
- Rita: I’m the hill, the fair, the calçadão, and the pixo. Roll the dice.
- Narrator: The odd breaks. The Casa falls. Funk and samba celebrate — the table belongs to the people now.
- **Bet:** Final duel → ending `povo-no-controle`.
